Discount Rate Trends by School

Scholarships as a share of gross tuition and fees for each program, FY2024 to FY2026 from audited note 15, with a straight-line least-squares fit for each. The fitted lines are extended five years back (to FY2019) and five years forward (to FY2031).

Discount rate with linear trend and projection

Solid points are audited. Solid lines are the fitted trend over the audited years; dashed lines are the projection. Turn on the check to compare the backward projection with values actually reported for FY2019 to FY2023.

Trend equations

Each line is y = a + b × (FY − 2025), where y is the discount rate in percent, a is the fitted rate in FY2025 and b is the change per year in percentage points. Centering on FY2025 keeps the intercept readable.

R² is the share of year-to-year variation the straight line explains. With only three audited years, a high R² shows the three points line up; it is not strong evidence that the trend will continue. A projection below zero is shown as 0%.

How it was calculated

Discount rate = scholarships ÷ (tuition + fees), for each program in note 15. The college total adds the three programs and matches the statements of activities.

Each trend is an ordinary least-squares line through the three audited years. Projections simply extend that line; they are not forecasts and do not account for enrollment, pricing or policy changes.

The backward check

  • College total: the squares are the discount rate reported to IPEDS for FY2019 to FY2023, calculated the same way. IPEDS gives 51.6% for FY2024, identical to the audit.
  • Undergraduate: the diamonds are an estimate from the Common Data Sets (list tuition times full-time undergraduates, less institutional grants). It is not audited and runs about 1.4 points above the audited rate in FY2024.
  • No comparable earlier figures exist for the graduate and law programs.
