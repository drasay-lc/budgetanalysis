Closure Risk

A published model of college closure and financial distress (Kelchen, Ritter and Webber, Philadelphia Fed, 2024), replicated on IPEDS data from 2002 to 2023, applied to Lewis & Clark and its 33 peers, and tested against closures that really happened. Model code and outputs: drasay-lc/closure-risk.
