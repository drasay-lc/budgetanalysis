Closure risk · model track record · IPEDS 2002 to 2023, closures to 2027

The model caught most small-college closures, but none at colleges Lewis & Clark's peers' size

Built with Claude Opus 5.5, November 2026, for Dan Rasay. Code and outputs: drasay-lc/closure-risk (src/closure_backtest.py).

This page asks whether the closure model from Kelchen, Ritter and Webber (Philadelphia Fed, 2024), as replicated for the peer analysis, would have warned about real closures. Every score here comes from a model that never saw the college it scores. A closure counts as flagged when the college sat in the riskiest tenth of private nonprofit four-year colleges in any of the scores covering its closure year.
