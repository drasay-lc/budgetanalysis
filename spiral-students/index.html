What the measures mean

Gross tuition is list-price tuition and fees for every student. Scholarships are the college's own aid, booked as a discount. Net tuition is what is left. The discount rate is scholarships divided by gross tuition.

Net operating result is the audited change in net assets from operating activities. It leaves out investment gains and gifts for buildings, so it is the closest thing a nonprofit has to operating profit.

First-year class is the number of first-time, first-year CAS students who started in the fall that opens the fiscal year (fall 2024 is FY2025). Returned for year two is the share of that class enrolled again the following fall.

Scope and caveats

  • College-wide: CAS, Law and the Graduate School together, the only level where an operating result is reported. CAS alone ran a 63% discount in FY2025 (audited note 15).
  • FY2021 and FY2022 results were lifted by pandemic spending cuts and federal grants (a peak of $7.0M in FY2022).
  • Inflation uses CPI-U, fiscal-year weighted; the 2025 index is approximate.
  • Tuition and the operating result cover the whole college; the student points cover CAS only, the undergraduate school.
  • Class sizes for FY2016 to FY2025 are the factbook's retention cohorts, which can differ by a few students from federal counts. Earlier years use federal (IPEDS) first-time enrollment.
