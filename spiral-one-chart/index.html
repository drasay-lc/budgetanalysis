How to read it

Each measure is divided by its own FY2015 value and multiplied by 100. A reading of 120 means 20% above FY2015; 80 means 20% below. That puts dollars, students and a percentage on one axis without a second scale.

FY2015 is the first year with both a retention rate and an audited operating result. The dollar lines are inflation-adjusted unless you switch to nominal.

First-year class is first-time, first-year CAS students who started in the fall that opens the fiscal year (fall 2024 is FY2025). Returned for year two is the share of that class enrolled again the next fall.

Scope and caveats

  • Tuition, scholarships and the operating result cover the whole college (CAS, Law and the Graduate School). The student points cover CAS only. CAS alone ran a 63% discount in FY2025 (audited note 15).
  • Class sizes for FY2016 to FY2025 are the factbook's retention cohorts; earlier years use federal (IPEDS) first-time enrollment. Retention starts with the FY2015 class.
  • FY2021 and FY2022 operating results were lifted by pandemic spending cuts and federal grants.
  • Inflation uses CPI-U, fiscal-year weighted; the 2025 index is approximate.
