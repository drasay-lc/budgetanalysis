About the data

Tuition, aid and operating results cover the whole college (the College of Arts & Sciences, the Law School and the Graduate School), the only level at which operating results are reported. First-year classes and retention cover the College of Arts & Sciences, the undergraduate school.

Sources: IPEDS finance and enrollment surveys; the college's CAS Trends factbook and Common Data Sets; audited financial statements published on the college's required reporting page; BLS CPI-U for inflation.
