Summary of sources

Seven tables, one for each kind of chart on the site, drawn from 18 public documents. Every row carries a numbered citation; the full list is at the bottom of the page.

Tuition and scholarships FY2000 to FY2025
Exact dollars for gross tuition, scholarships, net tuition and the discount rate, plus the inflation-adjusted values the charts draw. FY2000 to FY2024 come from the federal IPEDS Finance survey [1]; FY2025 comes from the 2025 audited financial statements [2].
Operating result FY2015 to FY2025
Operating revenue, expenses, result and margin, each year cited to its own audited statements [3] to [11] and [2]. The result is the change in net assets from operating activities.
First-year classes and retention Fall 1999 to fall 2024 classes
Entering class sizes and the number and share returning for a second year. The fall 2015 to fall 2024 classes come from the CAS Trends factbook [12], earlier class sizes from federal IPEDS enrollment data [14], and retention for the fall 2014 class from the Common Data Set [13].
Index, FY2015 = 100 FY2000 to FY2025
Computed from the three tables above, in both the inflation-adjusted and nominal versions that the one-chart animation switches between.
Discount rate by school FY2024 to FY2026, projected FY2019 to FY2031
Tuition, fees and scholarships for the undergraduate, graduate and law programs from note 15 of the FY2025 [2] and FY2026 [16] audited statements, the linear trend fitted to each program, and the reported rates used to check the backward projection: the college total from IPEDS [1] and an unaudited CAS estimate from the Common Data Sets [17].
Inflation adjustment FY2000 to FY2025
CPI-U annual averages [15] and the factor used to convert each year to FY2025 dollars. Fiscal years run June to May, so each year's index weights two calendar years. The 2025 value is approximate.

Checks. Every value in these tables was checked against the numbers the animations draw, and they match. Federal and audited figures agree to within a few dollars where they overlap: FY2016 net tuition is $82,194,736 in IPEDS and $82,194,732 in the audited statements [3].

Scope. Tuition, scholarships and the operating result cover the whole college (the College of Arts & Sciences, the Law School and the Graduate School). First-year classes and retention cover the College of Arts & Sciences only.

Downloads. Each table, and the source list, can be downloaded as a CSV from its section.
