Who is included

  • Willamette, Clark, Drew, Seattle Pacific and Lynchburg share Lewis & Clark's Carnegie class (Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate, Small). Stetson, Seattle University, Loyola New Orleans and Pacific University are in the Medium band of the same class.
  • More than half of Pacific University's students are in graduate and professional programs, so its institution-wide discount rate is far lower than the others.

Levels

  • All levels combines undergraduate, graduate and law tuition, because most of these schools' statements do not separate them.
  • Undergraduate only is available where a statement prints the split: Lewis & Clark from fiscal year 2024, Stetson from fiscal year 2018, and Drew and Lynchburg from fiscal year 2015.

How to read the figures

  • Drew, Seattle Pacific and Lynchburg come from the audited statements inside their federal single audit filings. Drew (fall 2018) and Lynchburg (fall 2021) cut their published tuition prices, so gross tuition and aid both step down while net tuition changes little. Seattle Pacific's gross tuition and aid also step down in fiscal year 2022 as printed.
  • Lewis & Clark, Stetson and Lynchburg (2024 and 2025) print net tuition and the aid amount, so gross is their sum. Missing years are gaps, not estimates.
  • Loyola's figures come from statement of activities extracts in its fact book, each attributed to the university audit. From fiscal year 2018 it also deducts externally funded scholarships, so its net tuition is lower than gross less institutional aid. Its 2024 and 2025 extracts print net tuition only.
  • Willamette's fiscal year 2021 uses the 12-month figures restated in its 2022 statement. Its Pacific Northwest College of Art merger adds to tuition from 2022, and Seattle University's 2025 includes under a month of Cornish College of the Arts.
