How the requirement was recorded

Each institution's current undergraduate catalog (2026-27, or 2025-26 where no newer edition is published) or its official general education page was read on November 3, 2026. Where a catalog blocks automated reading, the institution's own general education, registrar or language department pages were used. No third-party sites were used.

Where a school offers a choice of language options (Pacific Lutheran), the shortest option is used.

Segments

  • More than one year: three or more semesters from no background, for example a requirement met at the 201 level.
  • One year: two semesters, or three quarters.
  • Some students: the requirement applies only to some colleges or degrees, such as the College of Arts and Sciences or the BA.
  • Less than one year: one course, or language is one option in a menu of ways to meet a general education area.

Caveats

  • Reed's 2026-27 catalog could not be read, so its 2024-25 catalog was used; Reed's Common Data Set appears to list foreign language as required, which conflicts with the catalog.
  • Pacific Lutheran's current catalogs block automated reading, so its 2022-23 text was used and the current wording is unverified.
  • Ohio Northern rests on its general education policy and published degree plans.
  • Union's catalog says students choose how to distribute eight Perspectives courses, while its Complex Questions page says every student takes one in each; if the latter applies, one term of language is required.
