How the peer groups were chosen

All three groups appear on this page. Willamette is in groups 1 and 2 and is counted once, in group 1.

Group 1: L&C comparison group

The institutions Lewis & Clark's Office of Institutional Research uses in its College of Arts and Sciences comparison reports: regional peers in Oregon and Washington plus national liberal arts colleges, taken as published without additions or removals.

Gettysburg, Gonzaga, Kalamazoo, Kenyon, Occidental, Reed, Rhodes, Sarah Lawrence, Skidmore, St. Olaf, Union, University of Portland, University of Puget Sound, Whitman, Willamette.

Group 2: Carnegie class

Private institutions that share Lewis & Clark's 2025 Carnegie classification, Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate, Small (a liberal arts college with law and graduate schools), or sit in the adjacent Medium band, with endowments closer to Lewis & Clark's than most of group 1, and with audited statements or federal single audit filings available.

Clark, Drew, Loyola New Orleans, Lynchburg, Pacific University, Seattle Pacific, Seattle University, Stetson, Willamette (also in group 1).

Group 3: Smaller endowment

Private institutions with a liberal arts core plus professional or graduate programs, endowments of roughly $70 million to $300 million at fiscal year 2025, and audited statements available.

Capital, Goucher, Hamline, John Carroll, Ohio Northern, Pacific Lutheran, Roger Williams, Saint Mary's College of California, University of La Verne, University of Redlands.

How the requirement was recorded

Each institution's residence life or housing policy page, current housing contract, or student handbook was read on November 19, 2026. Only institutional pages were used as sources; where a third-party directory disagreed, the institution's own page was followed and the difference noted.

This is the housing (live-on) requirement, not the academic residency rule on credits earned at the institution.

Where a rule changed recently, the rule for students entering now or next is used, and the change is listed above.

Converting to semesters

  • Years: one academic year is two semesters. Quarter and trimester schools (Kalamazoo, Seattle Pacific, Seattle University, Union) are converted by academic year.
  • Credit thresholds: Lynchburg (85 credits) and Capital (90 credits for students entering fall 2027) release students at about the end of the third year, so both are shown as six semesters.
  • Age limits: some schools release students at 20, 21, 22 or 23 regardless of semesters; the count shown is for a student entering at a typical age.

Caveats

  • Lewis & Clark's six semesters is the new requirement; its catalog, Campus Living page and 2026-27 housing agreement still describe four semesters.
  • Whitman's catalog says students live in college-owned housing for eight semesters, but its Residence Life FAQ says juniors and seniors are not required to as of 2026-27; the Residence Life page was used.
  • Kalamazoo's Residence Life page (updated August 2026) requires juniors to live on campus, while its catalog still says only through sophomore year.
  • Sarah Lawrence and Hamline publish no live-on rule that could be found; both are moderate-confidence findings.
