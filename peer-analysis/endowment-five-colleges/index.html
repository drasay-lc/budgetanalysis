The comparison set

Five national liberal arts colleges, chosen for this comparison rather than from Lewis & Clark's published peer groups. All five have endowments five to eleven times the size of Lewis & Clark's and enroll mostly or only undergraduates, so endowment per student is far higher than at any school on the peer endowment page.

Williams College

Williamstown, Massachusetts. 2,067 undergraduates and 49 graduate students.

Swarthmore College

Swarthmore, Pennsylvania. Undergraduates only.

Wesleyan University

Middletown, Connecticut. The largest enrollment of the five, including 206 graduate students.

Bowdoin College

Brunswick, Maine. Undergraduates only.

Grinnell College

Grinnell, Iowa. Undergraduates only.

What is measured

Endowment net assets from the endowment note of each school's audited financial statements, at fiscal year end: May 31 for Lewis & Clark, June 30 for the other five. The FY2020 figure for each of the five is read from its FY2021 statements, which print June 30, 2020 as the prior year. The change combines investment returns, new gifts and spending, so it is not an investment return.

Each school's two years are on the same basis. The figures match each school's reported market value to the nearest $10 million or better where that was checked (NACUBO or federal IPEDS for Swarthmore, Bowdoin, Wesleyan and Grinnell).

Differences in what is counted

  • Wesleyan's endowment includes working capital funds, operating reserves invested alongside the endowment: $65.1M in 2020 and $57.4M in 2025. Without them its change is +48.6% rather than +45.1%.
  • Swarthmore and Williams exclude split-interest agreements from the endowment; Bowdoin, Wesleyan and Grinnell report outside trusts and split-interest assets elsewhere in their statements.
  • FY2026 audited statements were published for Lewis & Clark ($374.9M), Swarthmore ($3,028.2M) and Bowdoin ($3,552.4M) and are shown in the table only. Williams, Wesleyan and Grinnell had not posted FY2026 statements, so the comparison stops at FY2025.

Enrollment

  • Total headcount (undergraduate plus graduate) from each school's Common Data Set, item B1: fall 2025 for Swarthmore and Bowdoin, fall 2024 for Wesleyan and Grinnell, whose 2025-26 data sets were not yet posted.
  • Williams's figure is from its Fast Facts page, as of fall 2025, which draws on its Common Data Set; the data set itself could not be opened.
  • Wesleyan's headcount includes 741 part-time undergraduates reported as other students enrolled for credit (3,064 are full-time). Counting full-time undergraduates and graduate students only, its endowment per student would be higher.
