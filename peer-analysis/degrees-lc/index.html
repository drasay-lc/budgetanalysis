Every degree Lewis & Clark has reported to the federal IPEDS Completions survey, from the first file in 1984 to the latest in 2024: degrees by level, then bachelor's degrees by major. Each year is a graduating year (2024 means July 2023 to June 2024). Majors are counted by the federal program code (CIP) the college reported, grouped into today's L&C program names.

Source

U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Statistics, IPEDS Completions survey data files C1984_CIP through C2024_A (revised files where NCES published them), unit ID 209056, downloaded from the IPEDS Data Center on November 19, 2026.

Totals are the sum of men and women (1984 to 2007) or the grand total (2008 on). Summary rows (code 99) are excluded.

How majors are matched

  • The federal program codes changed in 1990, 2000, 2010 and 2020, and the college has recoded some majors (History moved from 45.08 to 54.01 in 2003; Sociology and Anthropology took its own code in 2013; Music and the languages used other codes from 2013 to 2015). Grouping by program name keeps those series continuous.
  • Lewis & Clark's undergraduate business degrees appear through 2007 and education degrees through 1991 (with one in 2003); World Languages is the combined major code 16.0101.
  • Interdisciplinary majors without their own code are reported under the closest code, so they do not appear separately.

Reading the counts

  • A graduate with two majors counts once under the first and again in the second-major figures, which IPEDS has collected only since 2001.
  • Certificates first appear in 2000; the large counts from 2000 to 2012 are post-baccalaureate and post-master's certificates from the graduate school.
  • Counts are what the college reported; IPEDS revises a year only when the college resubmits.
