Who is included

  • Kenyon, St. Olaf, Whitman, Union, Skidmore and Kalamazoo enroll undergraduates only (Skidmore has had a handful of graduate students), so their statement figures are undergraduate figures.
  • Reed, Occidental and Rhodes are about 99% undergraduate. Their figures are institution-wide and include a very small graduate program.
  • Gonzaga, Portland, Puget Sound, Willamette, Gettysburg and Sarah Lawrence are left out because their statements do not separate undergraduate tuition.

Lewis & Clark

Lewis & Clark's statements separate undergraduate tuition, fees and scholarships only from fiscal year 2024 (note 15 of the FY2025 and FY2026 audited statements). Earlier years combine the law and graduate programs and are left out here; the Carnegie peer page shows the college-wide figures.

How to read the figures

  • Net tuition is gross tuition less aid. Where a statement prints only net tuition and the aid amount (Whitman from 2020, Reed from 2019, Skidmore from 2019, Kalamazoo in 2025), gross is their sum. St. Olaf's aid is its funded plus unfunded scholarships, and its gross figure is tuition without other fees.
  • Skidmore, Kalamazoo and Rhodes come from the audited statements inside their federal single audit filings. From 2019 Skidmore prints aid to the nearest thousand dollars.
  • Older statements for Kenyon and St. Olaf are scanned images that could not be read, and several Reed years are not posted.
  • Aid definitions differ by school, so levels are less comparable than each school's own trend. Fiscal year 2021 reflects pandemic operations at several schools.
