Which colleges are included

The filter only hides rows; medians use the colleges shown.

Starting list

The 96 colleges ranked ahead of Lewis & Clark (tied at No. 97) in the U.S. News & World Report 2027 Best National Liberal Arts Colleges list, as used on the U.S. News leaders net tuition and yield page. The three service academies and Berea College, which report no tuition, were already left out there.

Kept here

The colleges whose net tuition after inflation, yield or both rose from FY2017 to FY2024, the longest of that page's three windows: 10 with both up, 7 with net tuition up only and 12 with yield up only.

Not kept

The 63 ranked colleges where both measures fell over that window. Starting from FY2018 instead would add 6 colleges and drop Reed; starting from FY2019 would add 10 and drop Bryn Mawr, Gustavus Adolphus, Occidental and Reed. All 10 colleges that grew both stay in under either start.

What is measured

Value of endowment assets at the end of the fiscal year from the federal IPEDS finance survey (FY2019 and FY2024, the latest year with finance data for every college), retrieved through the Urban Institute Education Data Portal. These are market values as each college reports them to IPEDS and can differ from endowment net assets in audited statements; Lewis & Clark's FY2024 IPEDS figure is $326.1M.

Why FY2019 to FY2024

  • The peer endowment page compares FY2020 and FY2025 from audited statements. IPEDS has no FY2025 finance data yet, so this page uses the same five-year span one year earlier, on one basis for every college.
  • Changes are before inflation and include gifts, investment returns and spending.

Cautions

  • Per student uses total fall 2023 headcount, so colleges with graduate or law students, Lewis & Clark among them, have more students to divide by than undergraduate-only colleges.
  • Thomas Aquinas College's endowment is about $31M, far smaller than the rest of the set.
  • Wesleyan's headcount rose from 3,271 in fall 2023 to 4,011 in fall 2024; this page uses fall 2023.
