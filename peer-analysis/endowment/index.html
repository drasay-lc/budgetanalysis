What is measured

Endowment net assets from the endowment note of each school's audited financial statements, at fiscal year end (May 31 or June 30). Endowment net assets include donor-restricted and board-designated funds and can differ from the market value a school reports to the national endowment survey.

Gettysburg's statements were not read; its figures are national endowment survey (NACUBO) market values, so it is the one school not on an audited basis.

Timing differences

  • Willamette moved its year end from May 31 to June 30 in 2021; its FY2020 figure is endowment net assets at the start of its restated 12-month FY2021, and its May 31, 2020 year-end figure was $249.7M. Pacific Northwest College of Art merged in on June 30, 2021.
  • Sarah Lawrence moved its year end from May 31 to June 30; Seattle University's FY2020 figure is at July 1, 2020.
  • Lewis & Clark's FY2026 endowment net assets were $374.9M (FY2026 audited statements); Skidmore's were $597.8M at May 31, 2026. Other peers' FY2026 statements were not yet read, so the comparison stops at FY2025.

Gaps and cautions

  • Loyola New Orleans publishes only statement of activities extracts with no endowment note, so it has no endowment figures.
  • Drew's and Saint Mary's endowments shrank over the period. Goucher's FY2024 endowment included $48.9M of gifts.
  • Enrollment for groups 1 and 2 is total headcount from each school's Common Data Set, fact book or profile page (fall 2025 where available; NCES College Navigator or a published compilation for a few), and for group 3 federal IPEDS fall headcount. Roger Williams has no enrollment figure because its statements include the law school, which reports separately.
