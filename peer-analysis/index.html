How the peer groups were chosen

The same three groups are used on every page in this section.

GroupSelection criterion and members
Group 1
L&C comparison group

The institutions Lewis & Clark's Office of Institutional Research uses in its College of Arts and Sciences comparison reports: regional peers in Oregon and Washington plus national liberal arts colleges, taken as published without additions or removals.

Gettysburg, Gonzaga, Kalamazoo, Kenyon, Occidental, Reed, Rhodes, Sarah Lawrence, Skidmore, St. Olaf, Union, University of Portland, University of Puget Sound, Whitman, Willamette.

Group 2
Carnegie class

Private institutions that share Lewis & Clark's 2025 Carnegie classification, Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate, Small (a liberal arts college with law and graduate schools), or sit in the adjacent Medium band, with endowments closer to Lewis & Clark's than most of group 1, and with audited statements or federal single audit filings available.

Clark, Drew, Loyola New Orleans, Lynchburg, Pacific University, Seattle Pacific, Seattle University, Stetson, Willamette (also in group 1, and counted there on the language page).

Group 3
Smaller endowment

Private institutions with a liberal arts core plus professional or graduate programs, endowments of roughly $70 million to $300 million at fiscal year 2025, and audited statements available.

Capital, Goucher, Hamline, John Carroll, Ohio Northern, Pacific Lutheran, Roger Williams, Saint Mary's College of California, University of La Verne, University of Redlands.

About the data

Tuition, aid and the discount rate come from each school's audited statement of activities and tuition notes, or the audited statements inside its federal single audit filing. Schools whose statements do not separate undergraduate tuition are left out of the undergraduate page rather than estimated.

Data tables and sources for every chart, with a numbered citation on every row and CSV downloads.
