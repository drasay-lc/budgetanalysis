    How the peer groups were chosen

    All three groups appear on this page. Willamette is in groups 1 and 2 and is counted once, in group 1.

    Group 1: L&C comparison group

    The institutions Lewis & Clark's Office of Institutional Research uses in its College of Arts and Sciences comparison reports: regional peers in Oregon and Washington plus national liberal arts colleges, taken as published without additions or removals.

    Gettysburg, Gonzaga, Kalamazoo, Kenyon, Occidental, Reed, Rhodes, Sarah Lawrence, Skidmore, St. Olaf, Union, University of Portland, University of Puget Sound, Whitman, Willamette.

    Group 2: Carnegie class

    Private institutions that share Lewis & Clark's 2025 Carnegie classification, Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate, Small (a liberal arts college with law and graduate schools), or sit in the adjacent Medium band, with endowments closer to Lewis & Clark's than most of group 1, and with audited statements or federal single audit filings available.

    Clark, Drew, Loyola New Orleans, Lynchburg, Pacific University, Seattle Pacific, Seattle University, Stetson, Willamette (also in group 1).

    Group 3: Smaller endowment

    Private institutions with a liberal arts core plus professional or graduate programs, endowments of roughly $70 million to $300 million at fiscal year 2025, and audited statements available.

    Capital, Goucher, Hamline, John Carroll, Ohio Northern, Pacific Lutheran, Roger Williams, Saint Mary's College of California, University of La Verne, University of Redlands.

    How matching works

    Each school's majors and minors were read from its current undergraduate catalog and matched to Lewis & Clark's 2026-27 College of Arts and Sciences majors and minors by program name and common synonyms, for example Spanish to Hispanic Studies, Communication to Rhetoric and Media Studies, and Sociology or Anthropology to Sociology and Anthropology.

    Same level means a major matched to a Lewis & Clark major or a minor to a minor. Other level only means the program matches a Lewis & Clark program offered only at the other level.

    Two ways to read overlap

    • Coverage (the charts): of Lewis & Clark's programs, the share the school also offers. This is the institution-level measure.
    • Share of the school's programs (the look-up panel): of the programs the school lists, the share with a Lewis & Clark equivalent. Schools with many professional programs score low here even when they offer everything Lewis & Clark does.

    Cautions

    • Schools that list each track, degree variant or interdepartmental combination as a separate program (Seattle Pacific, Stetson, Union, Loyola New Orleans, Capital, Seattle University) have inflated program counts.
    • Sarah Lawrence has no majors or minors in the usual sense; its listed areas of study are counted as majors, so its minor coverage is zero.
    • The matches are editable in the source Google Sheet ("LC Peer Majors and Minors Comparison"); this page reflects it as of November 4, 2026.
